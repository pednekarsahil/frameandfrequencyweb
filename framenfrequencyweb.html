Frame & Frequency is a post-production studio for video editing and audio mixing & mastering — built for work that needs to look sharp and sound sharp.

Our Approach

We start with the story. Every cut, every level, every master is chosen to serve the message — not to show off. Restraint over noise.

Our Delivery

Picture lock, colour, mix, master, delivery — we own the whole chain. Files arrive ready for broadcast, cinema, or streaming.

Workflow

From rushes
to final master.

01
Step One

Brief & Ingest

We review your footage, audio, and references — then agree on scope, format, and delivery specs before a single frame moves.

02
Step Two

Edit & Design

Picture cut, sound design, dialogue cleanup, and colour grade — shaped together so vision and audio stay in sync.

03
Step Three

Mix & Master

Full mix on calibrated monitors, then mastering to target loudness — podcast, streaming, broadcast, or cinema.

04
Step Four

Deliver & Archive

Masters, stems, captions, and platform-ready exports delivered. Project files archived for future revisions.
